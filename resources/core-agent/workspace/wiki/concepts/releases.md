# Release operations

Emergency releases need release-manager approval, and the current quarterly deployment freeze begins on December 15. [[source:raw/releases.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/releases.md}}|lines:2-2]]

Rollback starts when error rate exceeds 10 percent or a health check fails; the service owner owns it. [[source:raw/releases.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/releases.md}}|lines:3-3]]

The archived calendar starts the freeze on December 10, conflicting with December 15. [[source:raw/releases.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/releases.md}}|lines:4-4]]

An old note assigns rollback to the incident commander, conflicting with current service-owner ownership. [[source:raw/releases.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/releases.md}}|lines:5-5]]
