# Knowledge authority and citations

Persistent citations bind raw path, a 64-hex SHA-256, and lines, page, or region locator. [[source:raw/knowledge-contract.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/knowledge-contract.md}}|lines:2-2]]

Raw material is immutable evidence, approved Wiki Markdown is the fact layer, and SQLite FTS is only a candidate index. [[source:raw/knowledge-contract.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/knowledge-contract.md}}|lines:3-3]]

Source ownership controls corrections; a changed hash makes citations stale and lowers answer confidence until revalidated. [[source:raw/knowledge-contract.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/knowledge-contract.md}}|lines:4-4]]

Answers cite run-local Evidence IDs and every EvidenceItem traces to a successful knowledge tool call. [[source:raw/knowledge-contract.md|sha256:{{sha256:raw/knowledge-contract.md}}|lines:5-5]]
